      What this is

      No voice here was recorded. The text was parsed, its chapters found, its speakers attributed line by line, and each character given one of twenty-eight neural voices running on a single machine. The complete edition runs to sixty-three chapters.

      Known faults

      Two lines in the third passage are wrongly cast. They are left in.

      “Lizzy, remember where you are, and do not run on in the wild manner…” Heard in Mr. Bingley’s voice. It is Mrs. Bennet — the text tags it “cried her mother.”

      Across eight audited chapters sixty-three per cent of dialogue reaches a character. The rest falls to the narrator by design: where the evidence runs out, the line is not guessed.
